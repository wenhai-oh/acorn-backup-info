Acorn OCI Backups

Privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Acorn OCI Backups is a personal backup utility operated by its account owner.

Google data access and use

The utility uses the Google Drive API to store, list, read, restore and remove encrypted backup objects. It may read account identity and storage quota information to confirm the destination and available space. Its backup configuration targets a dedicated backup repository.

Google Drive authorization can grant broader access than that repository. The utility uses its access for backup operations and does not intentionally collect unrelated Drive files. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Photos or contacts.

Storage and protection

Selected application data is encrypted on the owner's server before upload to Google Drive. The server stores the credentials needed for scheduled backups. Backup contents and credentials are not published on this website.

Sharing

Backup data is processed on the owner's server and stored in the Google Drive account selected by the owner. It is not sold or used for advertising or training AI models. The utility does not send backup data to the website hosting provider.

Retention and deletion

Backup versions are retained and cleaned up according to the owner's configured policy. The owner can revoke the utility's access in Google Account settings and can remove the repository from Google Drive. Revoking access does not itself delete existing backup files.

This website

This static information site does not use analytics, forms or tracking cookies. Its hosting provider may process routine website access logs under its own privacy policy.

Contact

For questions, use the support email displayed on the utility's Google authorization screen.

Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
